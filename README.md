## <span style="color: #3498db;">📃 Overview</span>  
▫️ **MOGWSO** is an advanced optimization algorithm inspired by the hunting behavior of grey wolves, adapted for **multi-objective image segmentation tasks**.  
▫️ Designed for **remote sensing applications**, it efficiently handles **complex, high-dimensional optimization problems**.  
▫️ Combines **Grey Wolf Optimizer (GWO)** principles with **Pareto optimality** for robust segmentation.  

---

## <span style="color: #3498db;">📤 Key Features</span>  
🟦 **Multi-Objective Optimization** – Balances multiple fitness functions (e.g., entropy, edge preservation).  
🟦 **Adaptive Grey Wolf Hierarchy** – Alpha, Beta, Delta, and Omega wolves guide the search.  
🟦 **Efficient Convergence** – Fast and stable convergence with adaptive exploration-exploitation balance.  
🟦 **Remote Sensing Focus** – Optimized for **satellite & aerial imagery segmentation**.
